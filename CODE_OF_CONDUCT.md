# Code of Conduct

OMem follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Assume good intent, especially with new contributors.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.
- Give clear feedback with enough context to help someone improve.
- Respect maintainer time and project scope.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Repeated off-topic disruption
- Publishing private information without permission
- Any behavior maintainers judge harmful to the community

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or ban participants when needed to protect the project.

Report concerns through the private contact path listed in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) or by contacting a maintainer directly.
