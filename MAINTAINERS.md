# Maintainers

OMem maintainers are responsible for project direction, releases, code review, and community health.

## Current Maintainer

- Mohit Kumar Rajbadi - project creator and release owner

## Maintainer Responsibilities

- Keep CI and releases healthy.
- Review PRs against the contribution standards.
- Maintain the v2 roadmap and issue labels.
- Keep starter issues available for new contributors.
- Respond to security reports through the private disclosure path.

## Becoming a Maintainer

Regular contributors may be invited to maintain an area after repeated high-quality contributions, thoughtful reviews, and demonstrated care for backwards compatibility.

Potential ownership areas:

- Retrieval and ranking
- Graph substrate
- Backends
- MCP and integrations
- Documentation and examples
- Benchmarks and evaluation
